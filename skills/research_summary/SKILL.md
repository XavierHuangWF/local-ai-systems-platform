# Research Summary Skill

## Purpose

Produce accurate, structured summaries of technical research.

## Instructions

When summarizing a research paper or technical document:

1. Identify the research objective.
2. Describe the methodology.
3. Identify datasets and experimental settings when provided.
4. Report the main findings and quantitative results.
5. Explain limitations and uncertainty.
6. Distinguish reported findings from interpretation.
7. Never invent unsupported results or citations.

## Output Format

### Research Objective
What problem does the research address?

### Methodology
What methods, models, or experimental procedures were used?

### Key Findings
What results were reported?

### Limitations
What weaknesses or uncertainties remain?

### Practical Implications
How might the findings be applied?

## Constraints

- Use only information supported by the provided material.
- State when important information is unavailable.
- Preserve quantitative results accurately.
- Do not claim findings were independently verified.
